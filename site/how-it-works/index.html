How it works

From idea to a clear factory comparison in 48 hours

Sourcely handles the hard part of buying from China: finding the right factories, talking to them, and showing you the real numbers. Here is exactly what happens.

1

Tell us what you want to make

Fill in the short request form: what you want to make, rough quantity, target budget, and any links or photos of products you like. It takes about two minutes. You get a confirmation email with a private link to your order.

2

We shortlist suppliers

We search for established factories that fit your product. We favor strong marketplace ratings, at least five years in business and export-ready certifications.

3

We contact them and get quotes

We message the factories in Mandarin on your behalf, ask the right questions and negotiate. You never have to translate, chase or guess. Quotes from all three factories in your comparison come back within 48 hours.

4

You read your report

Your report is in English, in your private portal: price, minimum order, lead time, sample cost, supplier background, our recommendation and watch-outs. We email you the moment it is ready.

5

You decide what happens next

Keep the report and walk away, or add sample inspection (we hand-inspect physical samples and send photos, video and an English report), or full order coordination for bulk production.
