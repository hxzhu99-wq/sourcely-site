About

Built by someone who has been in your shoes

Sourcely helps small sellers outside China buy from Chinese factories without getting burned. We are a young company and we learn from every seller we work with.

Why we built it

Starting a product business is hard enough. Finding a factory you can trust, across a language gap and without insider contacts, is where a lot of good ideas stall. We built Sourcely to close that gap.

What we believe

  • No hype. We tell you what we found, including the problems.
  • Real numbers. Quotes, minimums and lead times side by side.
  • Your decision. We inform, you choose.
