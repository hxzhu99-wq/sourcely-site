FAQ

Questions, answered

Short, honest answers about how Sourcely works, what it costs and what we do not do.

What is Sourcely?

Sourcely is a sourcing service that helps small sellers outside China find and compare trustworthy Chinese factories. We contact suppliers for you in Mandarin, collect quotes and send you an English report, so you can decide before you place a bulk order.

How does Sourcely work?

You send a short request describing what you want to make. We shortlist suppliers, contact them, and deliver a comparison report for three factories within 48 hours. After that you choose: stop there, or continue with sample inspection or full order coordination.

How fast do I get my first report?

Within 48 hours of your request. That covers quotes from every factory in your comparison. On-site inspections depend on factory availability and cost extra.

How does Sourcely choose factories?

We shortlist established suppliers: strong marketplace ratings, at least five years in business and certifications that allow them to export. We also look at how quickly and clearly they communicate with us, because that predicts how an order will go.

What does the free report include?

Your first report is free: quotes from three factories for your product, compared side by side, with our recommendation and watch-outs. In return we ask for honest feedback and, only if you agree, permission to share your result as an anonymous case study.

Do I need an account?

No. Every request gets a private link to your order and report. You can optionally create a free account with just your email and a 6-digit code (no password) to keep all your orders in one place.

What happens after the free report?

You decide. You can keep the report and walk away, or move to a paid plan. Sample inspection means we hand-inspect physical samples and send photos, video and an English report. Full order coordination adds production tracking and bulk-order inspection coordination.

How much do paid plans cost?

Paid plans are a flat upfront fee with no surprise charges. We share the current price list when your free report is delivered, so you can decide with the numbers in front of you.

Are sample fees and international shipping included?

No. Sample fees and international shipping are paid by you, and you arrange your own freight. Sourcely handles the negotiation and the inspection, and we negotiate sample fees down where we can.

Do you inspect samples yourselves?

On paid plans, yes. We inspect physical samples by hand against the questionnaire you fill in, take detailed photos and video, and send you an English report. We do not rely only on pictures that factories send us.

Can you guarantee the quality of my bulk order?

No service can honestly promise that. We lower the risk by choosing established factories, inspecting samples and, on the Full Order plan, tracking production. You stay the buyer, and every decision is yours.

Is my information private?

Your request and report are visible only to you, through your private link, and to the Sourcely team. We do not sell your data. Read the privacy policy for details.
