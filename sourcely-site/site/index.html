FAQ

Quick answers

What is Sourcely?

Sourcely is a sourcing service that helps small sellers outside China find and compare trustworthy Chinese factories. We contact suppliers for you in Mandarin, collect quotes and send you an English report, so you can decide before you place a bulk order.

How does Sourcely work?

You send a short request describing what you want to make. We shortlist suppliers, contact them, and deliver a comparison report for three factories within 48 hours. After that you choose: stop there, or continue with sample inspection or full order coordination.

How fast do I get my first report?

Within 48 hours of your request. That covers quotes from every factory in your comparison. On-site inspections depend on factory availability and cost extra.

How does Sourcely choose factories?

We shortlist established suppliers: strong marketplace ratings, at least five years in business and certifications that allow them to export. We also look at how quickly and clearly they communicate with us, because that predicts how an order will go.

What does the free report include?

Your first report is free: quotes from three factories for your product, compared side by side, with our recommendation and watch-outs. In return we ask for honest feedback and, only if you agree, permission to share your result as an anonymous case study.
