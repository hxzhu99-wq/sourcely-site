Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

What we collect

When you request a report we collect your name, email, optional company name, a description of the product you want to make, and any budget details you share. If you sign in, we use your email to send you a one-time code.

How we use it

We use your information to do the work you asked for, to email you about your order, and to improve Sourcely. We contact factories on your behalf using the product details you give us, but we do not give factories your email address unless you ask us to.

Case studies

If you tick the optional case-study box, we may share your result without your name or any detail that identifies you. You can withdraw permission at any time by emailing us.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Workspace tools (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) and emails are sent through Google. We do not sell your data.

Your private link

Each order has a private link. Anyone who has the link can view that order, so please do not share it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show or delete your data at any time. Contact us through the request form, mention this request, and we will respond within 30 days.
