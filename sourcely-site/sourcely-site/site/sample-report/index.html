Our recommendation

Start with Factory C. The unit price is about 10% higher than Factory B, but you avoid tying up cash in 1,000 units before you know the product sells.

Watch-outs

  • Factory B was slow to answer detail questions. Expect delays if something goes wrong.
  • Factory A would not confirm logo placement in writing.
  • All prices exclude freight and import duties.

Suggested next steps

  1. Order samples from Factory C and one other factory.
  2. Have them hand-inspected before you commit to a bulk order.
  3. Confirm packaging and logo details in writing.
